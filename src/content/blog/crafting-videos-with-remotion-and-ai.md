## Crafting video content programmatically with Remotion and AI

Video is one of the most effective ways to communicate ideas—and as developers, we can build it the same way we build UI.

If you know the basics of React and have a bit of imagination, you can create astonishing business, educational, and entertainment videos programmatically, faster than you might think.

In this session we'll cover the basics of Remotion, explore what programmatic video unlocks for businesses and creators, and see how Claude Code and component libraries make the whole process faster. Expect live demos and practical takeaways you can use right away.
